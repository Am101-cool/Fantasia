Oh no! After countless days of training you have decided to engage in a selfish battle against the enchantress to try and become the strongest of the dark side but you are still an amateur and got too ahead of yourself. In a short amount of time you may have become part of the darkness but the enchantress was born in it. She senses your conniving intentions and before you can hit her with an attack, she casts a spell that banishes you into another realm. Try again :(
