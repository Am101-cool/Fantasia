Enchantress

Home | Author | Audience

The mysterious vibe of the old woman calls to you to approach her, but also due to her looks, you think that she is the best person to ask questions about this village since she seems like she has been around in this town for years. Just when you are about to speak to her she starts walking away into the nearby forest. You slowly follow her, still calling out to get her attention when she suddenly stops and transforms before you! A young beautiful woman turns around to now address you. She was not an old woman, she was an enchantress! Before you can even get a reaction out she introduces herself to you and provides you with a proposition. She says you are perfect and offers to teach you the ways of her dark magic. Do you accept this offer? (Accept Magic) Or decline and run away back to the publicity of the village? (Decline)
