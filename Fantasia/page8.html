Armor

Home | Author | Audience

Something about the armor is calling your name and as you pick it up, it is almost like you blackout and visions start to swarm your head of you in battle as you lead the army behind you to victory and protect the village and all its glory. The merchant snaps you back to reality and you decide to take the armor and thank him for it. He lets you know all the perks that come with it and how it is also fire resistant and as you are about to leave the shop he stops you to let you know you do not have much time and you need to start preparing for the Battle of Flames. Do you accept this challenge as fate has called for you? (Accept Battle) Or do you decline the responsibility? (Decline)
