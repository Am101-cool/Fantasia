Help Miners

Home | Author | Audience

Feeling out of place by not making the miners aware of your presence, you head over towards them to introduce yourself and offer them any assistance as they appear to be struggling. Despite being tired they resonate well with your kind gesture and hand you some tools and equipment to join them. After mining with them for some while, you all finally break through the area you were working on and are able to retrieve a great amount of frozen luxuries. While the rest of the team appear to be delighted by this discovery, you become conflicted on whether you want to share these findings with the others? (Share Findings) Or would you rather steal them for yourself? (Steal Findings)
