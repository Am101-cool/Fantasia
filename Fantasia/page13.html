The Hunt

Home | Author | Audience

You accept the Huntsman’s offer and ride off with him on his mission. After some time you realize you never asked him what exactly the mission entails and will consist of. He stops and turns to you and tells you the tale of a beast that roams the glaciers, terrorizing the villagers and eating their food supply. He explains to you that is the reason why the miners work so hard to gather all the artifacts they can find in order to trade them in for money and buy more supplies to keep them safe. The conversation ends abruptly when suddenly your attention is averted to a loud roar not too far down. It’s the beast you guys are looking for! Do you face the beast alongside the huntsman? (Face Beast) Or do you run away in fear? (Run Away)
