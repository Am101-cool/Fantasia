Decline Hunt

Home | Author | Audience

After politely declining the offer of the huntsman, he asks of you a favor. Since you will not go with him on his hunt, you are now tasked to watch over his pet baby dragon. You decide it is the least you can do, plus it is quite literally one of the cutest things you have ever seen. After some while of playing with the dragon you both become tired and take a little nap. The dragon wakes you up as he becomes uncomfortable, yearning and worrying about his companion. The dragon wishes to go after his partner. Do you let the huntsman’s pet follow after him? (Follow Huntsman) Or do you protect him from leaving following the huntsman's orders? (Protect Dragon)
