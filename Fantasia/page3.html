Ice Portal

Home | Author | Audience

You have finally chosen to go through the ice portal after contemplating your entire life before your eyes. The deep chilling blue and beautiful surrounding ice crystals won over your curiosity and you felt you had to know what lies within. As you come out of the other side you are met with an ice land that you have only seen in movies. It was as if the landscape upon you was made of glass because of how clear the ice was. As you look around you see further down a group of miners breaking down the ice, as you notice the frozen artifacts within it. Beside you you see a huntsman who appears to be preparing to leave on a mission. Do you explore and examine the ice/artifacts? (Explore Artifacts) Or do you approach and speak to the huntsman?
