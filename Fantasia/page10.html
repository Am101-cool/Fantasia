Dragon Egg

Home | Author | Audience

You cannot believe what is in front of you, a real dragon egg. You have always dreamed of having a mystical creature as a pet, but you couldn't because well they did not exactly exist where you were from. Here, however, in this realm, you can finally get what you have always wanted. You accept the offer of taking the egg and as you thank the merchant he stops you to let you know that something is coming for this village..something dark. As you stare at the egg in your hand you become conflicted between two things. Do you hatch the egg and train the dragon to protect you and the village? (Protect Village) Or do you hatch the egg and raise the dragon to be your lifelong companion and stay out of the village's affairs? (Raise Companion)
