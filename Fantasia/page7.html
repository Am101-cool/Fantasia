The miners seem to be struggling and exhausted, and you decide it is best to leave them alone as they need to keep their focus and synchronization to break down the thick layered ice. You approach the huntsman instead, but bracing yourself just in case he reacts angrily. Before you can fully approach the huntsman you are greeted first by a baby ice dragon who is his pet! The huntsman is ecstatic to see you and turns out to be a very sweet individual especially given the fact of how his dragon has taken a liking to you. Since he still has a mission to set forth on, he cuts the small talk and offers to join him on the hunt for the beast. Do you accept the offer and ride with the huntsman? (Accept Hunt) Or do you politely decline? (Decline Hunt)
