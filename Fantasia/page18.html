Spread Evil

Home | Author | Audience

You choose to agree with the enchantress and use the dark magic to spread evil and take over. The thought of immense power begins to fill you with greed and you start to have other plans. As the enchantress takes you back to her hideout and begins to train with you, you cannot help but feel bothered with all the thoughts that swarm your head. After your first day of training, you develop a plan. You can either use this training to your advantage and turn against the enchantress so that you can become the strongest (Turn Against) or you can practice your skills to perfect your craft and fight alongside her in battle (Fight Alongside).
