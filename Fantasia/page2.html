Fire Portal

Home | Author | Audience

After a long interval of indecisiveness you finally build up the courage to go through the fire portal. You fear in your head as you are about to walk through that you may get burned, but it just feels warm on your skin, as if you were almost invincible to the blazing flames. When you come out on the other side, you are in absolute awe about the gorgeous village that lies upon you. You run around to explore, you see baked goods that smell ever so sweet, kids are playing around, all types of animals and creatures you could only imagine roam all around you. But before you can get too excited, you want to know more about where you are. You see a merchant up ahead and what appears to be an elderly woman in a cloak reading some kind of book. Do you approach and speak to the merchant? (Merchant) Or do you approach and speak to the mysterious old woman? (Old Woman)
