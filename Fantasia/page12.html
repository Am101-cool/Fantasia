Dark Side

Home | Author | Audience

After a moment of deep consideration, you realize that in this reality you can be whatever you want to be, and find it more exciting to unlock a dark side of you you never knew you held within. As the excitement begins to fuel your body and the enchantress shares some of her magic with you, your entire look has now changed and you also hold the power to shapeshift and transform. The enchantress asks you how you feel and if you are ready to follow her lead, and just as you are about to reply, another thought enters your head. Do you agree with the enchantress and use the dark magic to spread evil and take over? (Spread Evil) Or do you remember the type of person you actually are, and instead decline and train with the dark magic to use for good? (Use for Good)
