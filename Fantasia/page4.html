Merchant

Home | Author | Audience

After finding the old woman rather creepy, you decide to approach and speak to the merchant. You aim to ask him questions on where you are, but he also seems to have some questions for you.. or rather, an offer. The merchant pleads with you to follow him to his shop in which you do, and there he presents before you a choice to make in order to start a new life. He reaches down behind his counter to where he lays in front of you a gold piece of armor, and an egg. However, this is not for food, it is a dragon's egg. Do you choose to examine the armor (Armor), or do you find yourself to be more interested in mystical dragon egg? (Dragon Egg)
